Spanish is spoken by over 500 million people in 20+ countries. The grammar is shared, but pronunciation, some forms of address and everyday vocabulary vary.

## Pronunciation

- **Distinción (most of Spain)**: *c* before e/i and *z* = *th*: [[cinco]] = "thinko". **Seseo (Latin America, Canaries, parts of Andalusia)**: both are *s*.
- **Yeísmo** (almost everywhere): *ll* and *y* sound the same. In Argentina and Uruguay they sound like *sh* / *zh*: [[calle]] ≈ "cashe".
- **Aspiration of s** (Caribbean, Andalusia, Southern Cone): final *s* becomes *h* or disappears: *los amigos* ≈ "loh amigo".
- **j** is a rough throat sound in Spain, a soft *h* in much of Latin America.

## Forms of address

| | Spain | Latin America |
|---|---|---|
| you all (friendly) | [[vosotros]] — *¿Venís?* | [[ustedes]] — *¿Vienen?* |
| you (singular, friendly) | [[tú]] | [[tú]] — or **vos** in Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay, Central America |

**Voseo**: *vos* replaces *tú*, with its own present-tense and command forms (stress on the ending):
| | tú | vos |
|---|---|---|
| hablar | hablas | [[hablás]] |
| comer | comes | [[comés]] |
| vivir | vives | [[vivís]] |
| ser | eres | [[sos]] |
| command: venir | ven | [[vení]] |
[[¿Vos sos de Buenos Aires?]] [[¿Qué querés?]]

**Usted** is used more widely in some countries (Colombia, Costa Rica) even with family.

## Past tenses

Spain uses the present perfect for today's events (*Hoy he comido*); most of Latin America prefers the preterite (*Hoy comí*).
