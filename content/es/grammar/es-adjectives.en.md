## Agreement

Adjectives **agree** with the noun in gender and number.

| ending | masc. sing. | fem. sing. | masc. pl. | fem. pl. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **-o** | [[alto]] | [[alta]] | [[altos]] | [[altas]] |
| **-e** | [[grande]] | grande | [[grandes]] | grandes |
| consonant | [[fácil]] | fácil | [[fáciles]] | fáciles |
| nationality (consonant) | [[español]] | [[española]] | [[españoles]] | [[españolas]] |

- [[El chico es alto.]] / [[La chica es alta.]] / [[Los chicos son altos.]] / [[Las chicas son altas.]]
- A mixed group → masculine plural: [[Juan y María son simpáticos.]]
- Adjectives in *-or* add *-a*: [[trabajador]] → [[trabajadora]].

## Position

Most adjectives go **after** the noun — the opposite of English:
[[un coche rojo]] (a red car), [[una ciudad grande]], [[un libro interesante]].

Some common adjectives usually come **before**: [[mucho]], [[poco]], [[otro]], numbers, and *bueno / malo* often do:
[[mucho tiempo]], [[otra vez]], [[un buen día]].

**Short forms** before a masculine singular noun:

| | |
|---|---|
| bueno → **buen** | [[un buen amigo]] |
| malo → **mal** | [[un mal día]] |
| primero → **primer** | [[el primer día]] |
| grande → **gran** (before any singular noun) | [[una gran ciudad]] = a great city |

> **grande** changes meaning with position: [[una ciudad grande]] = a big city; [[una gran ciudad]] = a great city.

## Muy

**muy** = very, and never changes: [[muy alto]], [[muy alta]], [[muy altos]].
