## My, your, his, our…

| owner | singular thing | plural things |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **mi** casa | **mis** casas |
| tú | **tu** casa | **tus** casas |
| él / ella / usted | **su** casa | **sus** casas |
| nosotros | **nuestro** coche / **nuestra** casa | **nuestros** coches / **nuestras** casas |
| vosotros | **vuestro** coche / **vuestra** casa | **vuestros** / **vuestras** |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | **su** casa | **sus** casas |

Possessive adjectives agree with the **thing owned**, not with the owner:

- [[mis padres]] — my parents (one owner, many parents → plural)
- [[nuestra madre]] — our mother (*nuestra* because *madre* is feminine)
- [[sus hijos]] — his / her / their / your children

**tu** (your) has no accent; **tú** (you) has one: [[Tú tienes tu libro.]]

## Su is ambiguous

*su* can mean his, her, its, their or your (usted/ustedes). If context isn't clear, use **de + person**:
[[Es su coche.]] → [[Es el coche de Ana.]] / [[Es el coche de ellos.]] / [[Es el coche de usted.]]

## Possession with de

Spanish has no *'s*: **the noun + de + owner**.
- [[el libro de Pedro]] — Pedro's book
- [[la casa de mis padres]] — my parents' house

## Family vocabulary

[[el padre]] / [[la madre]] — father / mother · [[los padres]] — parents · [[el hermano]] / [[la hermana]] — brother / sister · [[el hijo]] / [[la hija]] — son / daughter · [[el abuelo]] / [[la abuela]] — grandfather / grandmother · [[el tío]] / [[la tía]] — uncle / aunt · [[el primo]] / [[la prima]] — cousin · [[el esposo]] / [[la esposa]] — husband / wife · [[el novio]] / [[la novia]] — boyfriend / girlfriend

> Masculine plurals cover mixed groups: [[mis hermanos]] can mean "my brothers" or "my brothers and sisters".
