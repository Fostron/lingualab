Many Spanish verbs need a **specific preposition** — often different from English. Learn them as units.

## With a

[[aprender a]] + inf. (learn to) · [[ayudar a]] (help to) · [[empezar a]] / [[comenzar a]] · [[enseñar a]] (teach to) · [[invitar a]] · [[negarse a]] (refuse to) · [[acostumbrarse a]] (get used to) · [[atreverse a]] (dare to) · [[parecerse a]] (look like) · [[dedicarse a]] (do for a living) · [[oler a]] (smell of) · [[saber a]] (taste of)

## With de

[[acordarse de]] (remember) · [[olvidarse de]] (forget) · [[depender de]] (depend on!) · [[enamorarse de]] (fall in love with!) · [[tratar de]] (try to; be about) · [[dejar de]] (stop) · [[acabar de]] (have just) · [[cansarse de]] (get tired of) · [[darse cuenta de]] (realise) · [[despedirse de]] (say goodbye to) · [[quejarse de]] (complain about) · [[alegrarse de]] · [[aprovecharse de]] (take advantage of) · [[burlarse de]] (make fun of)

## With en

[[pensar en]] (think about!) · [[confiar en]] (trust) · [[insistir en]] (insist on) · [[fijarse en]] (notice) · [[tardar en]] (take time to) · [[quedar en]] (agree to) · [[convertirse en]] · [[entrar en]] (enter — *entrar a* in Latin America) · [[consistir en]] (consist of!)

## With con

[[soñar con]] (dream of!) · [[casarse con]] (marry!) · [[contar con]] (count on) · [[encontrarse con]] (bump into) · [[enfadarse con]] (get angry with) · [[acabar con]] (put an end to) · [[tener que ver con]] (have to do with)

## Watch out (English uses a different preposition or none)

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| depend **on** | depender **de** |
| fall in love **with** | enamorarse **de** |
| dream **of / about** | soñar **con** |
| marry someone | casarse **con** alguien |
| think **about** | pensar **en** |
| consist **of** | consistir **en** |
| look **for** | buscar (no preposition) |
| wait **for** | esperar (no preposition) |
| ask **for** | pedir (no preposition) |
| listen **to** | escuchar (no preposition) |
| look **at** | mirar (no preposition) |
