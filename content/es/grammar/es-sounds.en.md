Spanish spelling is very regular: once you know the rules, you can pronounce almost any word you see — and spell almost any word you hear. Click the examples to hear them.

## Vowels

Spanish has only **five vowel sounds**, and they are always short and pure (no "glide" like English *say* or *go*).

| letter | sound | example |
|---|---|---|
| a | like *a* in *father* | [[casa]] |
| e | like *e* in *bet* | [[mesa]] |
| i | like *ee* in *see* (short) | [[sí]] |
| o | like *o* in *not*, rounded | [[todo]] |
| u | like *oo* in *food* (short) | [[uno]] |

## Consonants that differ from English

| letter | how to say it | examples |
|---|---|---|
| **h** | always silent | [[hola]], [[hombre]] |
| **j**, **g** before e/i | a rough *h* from the throat | [[jamón]], [[gente]] |
| **g** before a/o/u | hard *g* as in *go* | [[gato]], [[amigo]] |
| **gue, gui** | hard *g*, the *u* is silent | [[guitarra]] |
| **c** before e/i, **z** | *th* in Spain, *s* in Latin America | [[cinco]], [[zapato]] |
| **c** before a/o/u, **qu** | *k* | [[casa]], [[queso]] |
| **ll**, **y** | like *y* in *yes* | [[calle]], [[yo]] |
| **ñ** | like *ny* in *canyon* | [[España]], [[mañana]] |
| **r** | a single tap of the tongue (like *tt* in American *butter*) | [[pero]] |
| **rr**, or **r** at the start of a word | a rolled, trilled *r* | [[perro]], [[rojo]] |
| **v** | same as **b** | [[vino]], [[bien]] |
| **d** between vowels | soft, like *th* in *this* | [[nada]] |

> Compare [[pero]] (but) and [[perro]] (dog): one tap vs. a trill changes the meaning.

## Where the stress goes

1. Words ending in a **vowel, -n or -s** are stressed on the **second-to-last** syllable: [[casa]], [[hablan]], [[libros]].
2. Words ending in **any other consonant** are stressed on the **last** syllable: [[hablar]], [[ciudad]], [[español]].
3. If a word breaks these rules, it gets a **written accent** on the stressed vowel: [[café]], [[lápiz]], [[música]], [[canción]].

Accents also distinguish words that are otherwise identical: [[tú]] (you) / [[tu]] (your), [[sí]] (yes) / [[si]] (if), [[él]] (he) / [[el]] (the), [[qué]] (what?) / [[que]] (that).

## Punctuation

Questions and exclamations are framed with an upside-down mark at the start: [[¿Cómo estás?]] [[¡Hola!]]
