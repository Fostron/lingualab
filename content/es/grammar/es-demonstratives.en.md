## This, that, that over there

Spanish has **three** distances:

| | near me | near you | far from both |
|---|---|---|---|
| masc. sing. | [[este]] libro | [[ese]] libro | [[aquel]] libro |
| fem. sing. | [[esta]] casa | [[esa]] casa | [[aquella]] casa |
| masc. pl. | [[estos]] libros | [[esos]] libros | [[aquellos]] libros |
| fem. pl. | [[estas]] casas | [[esas]] casas | [[aquellas]] casas |

The adverbs match: [[aquí]] (here) ↔ *este*, [[ahí]] (there, near you) ↔ *ese*, [[allí]] / [[allá]] (over there) ↔ *aquel*.

- [[Este café está frío.]] — This coffee is cold.
- [[¿Cuánto cuesta esa camisa?]] — How much is that shirt (the one you're holding)?
- [[Aquella montaña es muy alta.]] — That mountain over there is very high.

In everyday speech **ese** is used for most "that"; **aquel** is for clearly distant things or the distant past: [[en aquella época]] — in those days.

## As pronouns

The same words work alone, meaning *this one / that one*: [[Quiero este, no ese.]] — I want this one, not that one. (Old spelling with accents — *éste, ése* — is no longer required.)

## Neuter: esto, eso, aquello

For an unknown thing, an idea or a whole situation (never for a specific noun):
- [[¿Qué es esto?]] — What is this?
- [[Eso es verdad.]] — That's true.
- [[Por eso.]] — That's why.

Time expressions: [[este año]], [[esta semana]], [[esta noche]] (tonight), [[esta mañana]] (this morning).
