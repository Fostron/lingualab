## Perfect subjunctive: haya hecho

**haber in the present subjunctive + past participle**:

| | |
|---|---|
| (que) yo **haya** | terminado |
| tú **hayas** | comido |
| él/ella/Ud. **haya** | vivido |
| nosotros **hayamos** | hecho |
| vosotros **hayáis** | dicho |
| ellos/Uds. **hayan** | vuelto |

It is the subjunctive counterpart of the **present perfect** (*he hecho*) and also of a **future perfect** idea (*will have done*). Use it when a subjunctive trigger in the **present** refers to a **completed** action:

- [[Me alegro de que hayas venido.]] — I'm glad you've come / you came.
- [[No creo que haya terminado todavía.]] — I don't think he has finished yet.
- [[Es posible que se hayan perdido.]] — They may have got lost.
- [[Espero que lo hayas pasado bien.]] — I hope you had a good time.
- [[Siento que no te haya gustado.]]
- [[Cuando hayas terminado, llámame.]] — When you've finished, call me.
- [[Busco a alguien que haya vivido en Japón.]]

## Compare

| | |
|---|---|
| [[Espero que venga.]] | I hope he comes (now / future). |
| [[Espero que haya venido.]] | I hope he has come / came. |
| [[Esperaba que viniera.]] | I hoped he would come (past main verb → imperfect subjunctive). |

> The perfect subjunctive goes with a main verb in the **present, future, present perfect or imperative**.
