## Every noun has a gender

In Spanish every noun is **masculine** or **feminine** — even objects and ideas. The gender decides the form of articles and adjectives, so **always learn a noun together with its article**: not *casa* but [[la casa]].

| usually masculine | usually feminine |
|---|---|
| ending in **-o**: [[el libro]], [[el perro]] | ending in **-a**: [[la casa]], [[la mesa]] |
| ending in **-or**: [[el ordenador]] | ending in **-ción, -sión**: [[la canción]] |
| ending in **-aje**: [[el viaje]] | ending in **-dad, -tad**: [[la ciudad]], [[la libertad]] |
| | ending in **-umbre**: [[la costumbre]] |

Important exceptions you'll meet early:

- [[el día]], [[el mapa]], [[el problema]], [[el idioma]], [[el sistema]], [[el programa]] — masculine despite **-a** (many words in *-ma* are Greek and masculine).
- [[la mano]], [[la foto]] (= fotografía), [[la radio]], [[la moto]] — feminine despite **-o**.
- Nouns ending in **-e** or another consonant can be either: [[el coche]], [[la noche]], [[el hotel]], [[la flor]]. Just learn them.

For people, the gender usually follows the person: [[el niño]] / [[la niña]], [[el profesor]] / [[la profesora]], [[el estudiante]] / [[la estudiante]].

## The definite article — "the"

| | singular | plural |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | **el** libro | **los** libros |
| feminine | **la** casa | **las** casas |

A mixed group is masculine: [[los padres]] = the parents (father + mother).

> **el agua**: feminine nouns that begin with a **stressed a-** take *el* in the singular to avoid two *a* sounds clashing: [[el agua]], [[el alma]], [[el área]]. They are still feminine: [[el agua fría]], [[las aguas]].

Spanish uses *the* more than English — for general statements and with abstract nouns: [[Me gusta el café.]] (I like coffee), [[La vida es bella.]] (Life is beautiful).

## Plural

| ending | rule | example |
|---|---|---|
| vowel | add **-s** | [[libro]] → [[libros]] |
| consonant | add **-es** | [[ciudad]] → [[ciudades]], [[papel]] → [[papeles]] |
| **-z** | **-z → -ces** | [[lápiz]] → [[lápices]], [[vez]] → [[veces]] |
| **-ión** | add -es, drop the accent | [[canción]] → [[canciones]] |

## Contractions

**a + el = al**, **de + el = del**: [[Voy al parque.]] [[Es el coche del profesor.]] (but *a la*, *de la* don't contract).
