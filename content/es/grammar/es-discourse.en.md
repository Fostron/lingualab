Discourse markers organise speech and writing and show your attitude. At B2 you should be able to use a range of them, not just *y, pero, porque*.

## Structuring a text or argument

[[en primer lugar]] (firstly) · [[por un lado… por otro (lado)]] · [[en segundo lugar]] · [[además]] / [[asimismo]] (formal: likewise) · [[por último]] / [[finalmente]] · [[en conclusión]] / [[en resumen]] / [[en definitiva]]

## Contrast and concession

[[sin embargo]] / [[no obstante]] (formal) — however · [[en cambio]] — on the other hand · [[por el contrario]] — on the contrary · [[aun así]] — even so · [[de todas formas / maneras]] / [[de todos modos]] — anyway, in any case · [[ahora bien]] — now then, however

## Cause and consequence

[[por lo tanto]] / [[por consiguiente]] / [[en consecuencia]] — therefore · [[así pues]] — so, thus · [[de ahí que]] + subj. — hence: [[Llovía mucho; de ahí que no saliéramos.]] · [[dado que]] / [[puesto que]] / [[ya que]] — given that

## Reformulating and clarifying

[[es decir]] · [[o sea]] (informal) · [[mejor dicho]] (or rather) · [[en otras palabras]] · [[dicho de otro modo]]

## Giving examples and emphasis

[[por ejemplo]] · [[como]] · [[en concreto]] (specifically) · [[de hecho]] (in fact) · [[en realidad]] (actually) · [[sobre todo]] (above all) · [[incluso]] (even) · [[ni siquiera]] (not even)

## Conversation markers

[[bueno]] (well…) · [[pues]] (well, so) · [[vale]] (OK — Spain) · [[mira]] / [[oye]] (look / listen) · [[la verdad es que…]] (to be honest) · [[por cierto]] (by the way) · [[total, que…]] (so anyway…) · [[es que…]] (the thing is…) · [[¿no?]] / [[¿verdad?]] (isn't it?) · [[en fin]] (anyway, oh well) · [[a ver]] (let's see)

> **actualmente** = currently (not "actually") · **en realidad / de hecho** = actually · **eventualmente** = possibly, occasionally (not "eventually" → *finalmente, al final*).
