Idioms are where a language shows its personality. These are widely used across the Spanish-speaking world (a few are regional — noted).

## With the body

| idiom | meaning |
|---|---|
| [[tomar el pelo]] | to pull someone's leg — *¿Me estás tomando el pelo?* |
| [[no tener pelos en la lengua]] | to speak one's mind |
| [[costar un ojo de la cara]] | to cost an arm and a leg |
| [[echar una mano]] | to lend a hand |
| [[meter la pata]] | to put one's foot in it |
| [[estar hasta las narices]] | to be fed up |
| [[no pegar ojo]] | not to sleep a wink |
| [[tener buena / mala cara]] | to look well / unwell |
| [[ser uña y carne]] | to be inseparable |
| [[hablar por los codos]] | to talk non-stop |
| [[tener la cabeza en las nubes]] | to have one's head in the clouds |
| [[no tener ni pies ni cabeza]] | to make no sense at all |

| [[estar en su salsa]] | to be in one's element |
