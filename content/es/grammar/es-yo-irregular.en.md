## Verbs irregular only in the yo form

Many common verbs are regular in the present **except for yo**:

| infinitive | yo | rest (regular) |
|---|---|---|
| [[hacer]] — to do, make | **hago** | haces, hace, hacemos… |
| [[poner]] — to put | **pongo** | pones, pone… |
| [[salir]] — to go out, leave | **salgo** | sales, sale… |
| [[traer]] — to bring | **traigo** | traes, trae… |
| [[caer]] — to fall | **caigo** | caes, cae… |
| [[saber]] — to know | **sé** | sabes, sabe… |
| [[ver]] — to see | **veo** | ves, ve… |
| [[dar]] — to give | **doy** | das, da, damos, dais, dan |
| [[conocer]] — to know (people/places) | **conozco** | conoces, conoce… |
| [[conducir]] — to drive | **conduzco** | conduces… |
| [[traducir]] — to translate | **traduzco** | traduces… |

Verbs in **-cer / -cir** after a vowel take **-zco**: *conozco, parezco, ofrezco, conduzco*.

## Irregular yo + stem change

| tener | venir | decir | oír |
|---|---|---|---|
| **tengo** | **vengo** | **digo** | **oigo** |
| tienes | vienes | dices | oyes |
| tiene | viene | dice | oye |
| tenemos | venimos | decimos | oímos |
| tenéis | venís | decís | oís |
| tienen | vienen | dicen | oyen |

## Saber vs conocer

Both mean *to know*, but:

| saber | conocer |
|---|---|
| facts, information: [[Sé su nombre.]] [[No sé dónde está.]] | people: [[Conozco a tu hermano.]] |
| how to do something (+ infinitive): [[Sé nadar.]] — I can swim. | places: [[¿Conoces Madrid?]] — Have you been to Madrid? |
| | be familiar with something: [[Conozco esta canción.]] |

> With people, **conocer a**: the "personal a" goes before a person who is a direct object: [[Conozco a María.]]
