## Guessing with the future and conditional

Spanish uses tenses to express **conjecture** — where English says *probably, must, I wonder, I guess*.

| guess about… | tense | example |
|---|---|---|
| the **present** | simple future | [[¿Dónde estará Juan? — Estará en casa.]] — Where can Juan be? — He's probably at home. |
| the **recent past** | future perfect | [[Habrá perdido el tren.]] — He must have missed the train. |
| the **past** | conditional | [[Serían las diez cuando llegó.]] — It must have been around ten when he arrived. |
| the past before that | conditional perfect | [[Ya habría salido.]] — He had probably already left. |

- [[¿Qué hora es? — No sé, serán las cinco.]]
- [[Tendrá unos cuarenta años.]] — He must be about forty.
- [[¿Quién llamaría tan tarde?]] — Who could have called so late?
- [[Estarías cansado.]] — You must have been tired.

## Other ways to express probability

| | |
|---|---|
| **deber de** + infinitive | [[Debe de ser muy tarde.]] — It must be very late. |
| **a lo mejor** + indicative | [[A lo mejor está enfermo.]] |
| **quizá(s) / tal vez** + subj. / ind. | [[Quizás esté enfermo.]] |
| **probablemente / seguramente** | [[Seguramente llegará tarde.]] |
| **supongo que / me imagino que** + ind. | [[Supongo que tienes razón.]] |
| **puede (ser) que** + subj. | [[Puede que llueva.]] |

> *deber* + infinitive = obligation (*must, should*); *deber **de*** + infinitive = probability. In everyday speech many people use *deber* for both.
