## Stem-changing ("boot") verbs

In many common verbs, the **stressed vowel of the stem changes** in the present tense. The change happens in every form **except nosotros and vosotros** (where the stress falls on the ending). If you draw a line around the changing forms in the table, it looks like a boot — hence the name.

| | e → ie: querer | o → ue: poder | e → i: pedir |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | qu**ie**ro | p**ue**do | p**i**do |
| tú | qu**ie**res | p**ue**des | p**i**des |
| él/ella/Ud. | qu**ie**re | p**ue**de | p**i**de |
| nosotros | queremos | podemos | pedimos |
| vosotros | queréis | podéis | pedís |
| ellos/Uds. | qu**ie**ren | p**ue**den | p**i**den |

**e → ie**: [[querer]] (want), [[pensar]] (think), [[empezar]] (begin), [[entender]] (understand), [[preferir]] (prefer), [[cerrar]] (close), [[perder]] (lose), [[sentir]] (feel), [[tener]] and [[venir]] (+ irregular *yo*).

**o → ue**: [[poder]] (can), [[dormir]] (sleep), [[volver]] (return), [[encontrar]] (find), [[contar]] (tell/count), [[costar]] (cost), [[recordar]] (remember), [[morir]] (die). **jugar** is the only **u → ue**: [[juego]], [[juegas]].

**e → i** (only *-ir* verbs): [[pedir]] (ask for), [[repetir]] (repeat), [[servir]] (serve), [[seguir]] (follow — *sigo*), [[vestirse]] (get dressed).

- [[¿Puedes repetir, por favor?]] — Can you repeat, please?
- [[No entiendo.]] — I don't understand.
- [[¿Cuánto cuesta?]] — How much does it cost?
- [[Empiezo a trabajar a las nueve.]]
- [[Pienso que tienes razón.]]
- [[Pedimos dos cafés.]]

## Useful patterns

- **querer / poder / preferir + infinitive**: [[Quiero aprender español.]] [[¿Puedo pasar?]]
- **empezar a + infinitive**: [[Empieza a llover.]]
- **volver a + infinitive** = to do again: [[Vuelvo a llamar mañana.]]
- **pensar en** = to think about: [[Pienso en ti.]]

> The dictionary won't always warn you — learn these verbs with their *yo* form: *querer (quiero), poder (puedo), pedir (pido)*.
