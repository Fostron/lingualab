## Adverbs in -mente

Take the **feminine** form of the adjective and add **-mente** (= English *-ly*):

| adjective | adverb |
|---|---|
| rápido → rápida | [[rápidamente]] |
| lento → lenta | [[lentamente]] |
| fácil | [[fácilmente]] |
| tranquilo → tranquila | [[tranquilamente]] |
| real | [[realmente]] |
| claro → clara | [[claramente]] |

The adjective keeps its written accent: *rápidamente, fácilmente*.
With two adverbs in a row, only the last takes *-mente*: [[Habla clara y lentamente.]]

Many everyday adverbs don't end in *-mente*: [[bien]], [[mal]], [[despacio]] (slowly), [[deprisa]] (quickly), [[rápido]] (also used as an adverb: *¡Ven rápido!*), [[pronto]] (soon), [[tarde]], [[temprano]], [[casi]] (almost), [[solo]] (only), [[ya]], [[todavía]].

## Muy or mucho?

| **muy** + adjective / adverb | **mucho** + noun / after a verb |
|---|---|
| [[muy bonito]], [[muy bien]], [[muy tarde]] | [[mucho dinero]], [[muchas personas]] |
| | [[Trabajo mucho.]] — I work a lot. |

*mucho* before a noun agrees (*mucha agua, muchos libros*); after a verb it never changes.
Exceptions: **mejor, peor, mayor, menor, más, menos** take *mucho*: [[mucho mejor]], [[mucho más caro]].

## Degree

[[demasiado]] (too, too much): [[Es demasiado caro.]] [[Comes demasiado.]] · [[bastante]] (quite, enough): [[Es bastante difícil.]] · [[un poco]] (a bit): [[Estoy un poco cansado.]] · [[nada]] (not at all): [[No es nada fácil.]]

## Frequency

[[siempre]] · [[casi siempre]] · [[normalmente]] / [[generalmente]] · [[a menudo]] (often) · [[a veces]] (sometimes) · [[de vez en cuando]] (from time to time) · [[casi nunca]] · [[nunca]]

Position: frequency adverbs usually go before the verb or at the end: [[A veces voy al cine.]] [[Voy al cine a veces.]]
