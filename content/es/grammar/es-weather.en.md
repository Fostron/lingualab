## Talking about the weather

[[¿Qué tiempo hace?]] — What's the weather like? (*el tiempo* = both *time* and *weather*)

Spanish uses three verbs for weather:

**hacer** (+ noun) — for most general conditions:
| | |
|---|---|
| [[Hace calor.]] | It's hot. |
| [[Hace frío.]] | It's cold. |
| [[Hace sol.]] | It's sunny. |
| [[Hace viento.]] | It's windy. |
| [[Hace buen tiempo.]] / [[Hace mal tiempo.]] | The weather is good / bad. |
| [[Hace 30 grados.]] | It's 30 degrees. |

Because *calor, frío, sol* are nouns, "very" is **mucho**: [[Hace mucho calor.]] [[Hace mucho frío.]]

**estar** (+ adjective / gerund):
[[Está nublado.]] — It's cloudy. [[Está lloviendo.]] — It's raining (right now). [[Está despejado.]] — It's clear.

**hay** (+ noun): [[Hay niebla.]] (fog), [[Hay tormenta.]] (storm), [[Hay nubes.]]

**Weather verbs** used only in the 3rd person singular: [[llover]] → [[llueve]] (it rains), [[nevar]] → [[nieva]] (it snows).
[[En Londres llueve mucho.]] [[En invierno nieva en las montañas.]]

## Seasons

[[la primavera]] (spring) · [[el verano]] (summer) · [[el otoño]] (autumn) · [[el invierno]] (winter)
[[En verano hace mucho calor.]]

## People vs weather

| the weather | a person | a thing |
|---|---|---|
| [[Hace frío.]] | [[Tengo frío.]] — I'm cold. | [[El agua está fría.]] — The water is cold. |
| [[Hace calor.]] | [[Tengo calor.]] — I'm hot. | [[La sopa está caliente.]] — The soup is hot. |
