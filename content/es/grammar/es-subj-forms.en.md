## What is the subjunctive?

The **indicative** states facts. The **subjunctive** (*subjuntivo*) presents actions as wished-for, doubted, felt about, or not yet real. It appears mostly in a **subordinate clause after que**, when the main clause expresses influence, emotion, doubt or unreality — and the subjects of the two clauses are different.

[[Sé que vienes.]] — I know you're coming (fact → indicative).
[[Quiero que vengas.]] — I want you to come (wish → subjunctive).

## Forming the present subjunctive

Start from the **yo form of the present indicative**, drop the **-o**, and add the "opposite" vowel endings:

| | -ar: hablar (hablo) | -er: comer (como) | -ir: vivir (vivo) |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | habl**e** | com**a** | viv**a** |
| tú | habl**es** | com**as** | viv**as** |
| él/ella/Ud. | habl**e** | com**a** | viv**a** |
| nosotros | habl**emos** | com**amos** | viv**amos** |
| vosotros | habl**éis** | com**áis** | viv**áis** |
| ellos/Uds. | habl**en** | com**an** | viv**an** |

Because it starts from *yo*, irregular *yo* forms carry over:
tener (tengo) → [[tenga]], hacer (hago) → [[haga]], decir (digo) → [[diga]], conocer (conozco) → [[conozca]], salir (salgo) → [[salga]], venir (vengo) → [[venga]], poner → [[ponga]], traer → [[traiga]], oír → [[oiga]], ver → [[vea]].

**Stem-changers** change in the same "boot" forms; -ir stem-changers also change in *nosotros/vosotros*: querer → *quiera, queramos*; poder → *pueda, podamos*; pedir → *pida, pidamos*; dormir → *duerma, durmamos*; sentir → *sienta, sintamos*.

**Spelling**: -car → **-que** (buscar → [[busque]]), -gar → **-gue** (pagar → [[pague]]), -zar → **-ce** (empezar → [[empiece]]).
