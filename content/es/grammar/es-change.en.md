English *become / get / turn* has several Spanish equivalents depending on the **kind of change**.

| verb | type of change | examples |
|---|---|---|
| **ponerse** + adjective | quick, temporary change of mood, appearance, health | [[Se puso rojo.]] — He went red. [[Me pongo nervioso.]] [[Se puso enferma.]] [[Se pone triste cuando llueve.]] |
| **volverse** + adjective | sudden but lasting change of character, often negative | [[Se volvió loco.]] [[Se ha vuelto muy egoísta.]] |
| **hacerse** + noun / adjective | change through effort or gradual process; profession, religion, ideology | [[Se hizo rico.]] [[Se hizo médico.]] [[Nos hicimos amigos.]] [[Se está haciendo tarde.]] |
| **llegar a ser** | becoming something after a long process | [[Llegó a ser presidente.]] |
| **convertirse en** + noun | transformation into something different | [[El agua se convirtió en hielo.]] [[Se convirtió en un problema.]] |
| **quedarse** + adjective | change as the result of an event, often loss | [[Se quedó ciego.]] [[Me quedé sorprendido.]] [[Se quedó sin trabajo.]] [[Se quedó embarazada.]] |

Notes:
- **ponerse** cannot be followed by a noun: *se puso médico* is wrong → *se hizo médico*.
- **hacerse mayor** = to grow up / get older; **envejecer** = to age.
- Many changes have their own verb: [[enfadarse]] (get angry), [[cansarse]] (get tired), [[aburrirse]] (get bored), [[enamorarse]] (fall in love), [[enfermar]] (fall ill), [[adelgazar]] (lose weight), [[engordar]] (put on weight), [[envejecer]] (get old), [[enriquecerse]] (get rich), [[mejorar]] / [[empeorar]].

> Choose by asking: is it a passing state (ponerse), a deep personality change (volverse), an achievement (hacerse / llegar a ser), a transformation (convertirse en) or a result left behind (quedarse)?
