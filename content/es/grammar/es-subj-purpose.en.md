## Para que — so that (always subjunctive)

**para + infinitive** when the subject is the same; **para que + subjunctive** when it changes:
- [[Estudio para aprobar.]] — I study (in order) to pass.
- [[Te lo explico para que lo entiendas.]] — I'm explaining it so that you understand.
- [[Hablo despacio para que me entiendan.]]

Similar: **a fin de que** (formal), **de modo que / de manera que** (when they mean *so that* — with a result already happened they take the indicative).

## Sin que — without (always subjunctive)

- [[Salió sin decir nada.]] — He left without saying anything (same subject).
- [[Salió sin que nadie lo viera.]] — He left without anyone seeing him.
- [[No puedo hacer nada sin que mi madre se entere.]]

## Aunque — although / even if

**aunque + indicative**: a known fact — *although*:
[[Aunque llueve, voy a salir.]] — Although it's raining (it is), I'm going out.

**aunque + subjunctive**: a possibility or something you don't accept as relevant — *even if*:
[[Aunque llueva, voy a salir.]] — Even if it rains, I'm going out.
[[Aunque sea caro, lo compro.]] — Even if it's expensive (I don't care), I'll buy it.

## A pesar de (que)

**a pesar de + noun / infinitive**: [[A pesar del frío, salimos.]] [[A pesar de estar cansado, siguió trabajando.]]
**a pesar de que** + indicative (fact) / subjunctive (hypothesis), like *aunque*.

## Other conjunctions that always take the subjunctive

**antes de que** (before), **con tal de que** (provided that), **a menos que / a no ser que** (unless), **en caso de que** (in case) — you'll practise these at B2.
