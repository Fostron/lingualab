## -er and -ir verbs

The endings are almost the same: **-er** and **-ir** verbs differ only in the *nosotros* and *vosotros* forms.

| | comer (to eat) | vivir (to live) |
|---|---|---|
| yo | [[como]] | [[vivo]] |
| tú | [[comes]] | [[vives]] |
| él / ella / usted | [[come]] | [[vive]] |
| nosotros | [[comemos]] | [[vivimos]] |
| vosotros | [[coméis]] | [[vivís]] |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | [[comen]] | [[viven]] |

**Regular -er verbs:** beber, leer, aprender, comprender, correr, creer, vender, deber.
**Regular -ir verbs:** vivir, escribir, abrir, recibir, subir, decidir, compartir, existir.

- [[¿Dónde vives?]] — Where do you live?
- [[Leo un libro cada semana.]]
- [[Mis hijos comen mucha fruta.]]
- [[Escribimos correos en español.]]
- [[No comprendo la pregunta.]]
- [[Debes descansar.]] — You should rest. (*deber* + infinitive = should / must)

## Summary of present endings

| | -ar | -er | -ir |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | -o | -o | -o |
| tú | -as | -es | -es |
| él/ella/Ud. | -a | -e | -e |
| nosotros | -amos | -emos | **-imos** |
| vosotros | -áis | -éis | **-ís** |
| ellos/Uds. | -an | -en | -en |

> A quick way to remember: -er/-ir verbs swap the **a** of -ar verbs for **e**.

**leer** and **creer** are regular in the present: [[leo]], [[lees]], [[creo]], [[crees]]. (*Ver* is slightly irregular: **veo**, ves, ve…)
