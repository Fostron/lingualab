## Estar — the other "to be"

| | |
|---|---|
| yo **estoy** | nosotros **estamos** |
| tú **estás** | vosotros **estáis** |
| él / ella / usted **está** | ellos / ellas / ustedes **están** |

Use **estar** for:

- **location** (where something/someone is): [[Estoy en casa.]] [[¿Dónde está el baño?]] [[Madrid está en España.]]
- **states and conditions** (how something is *right now*): [[Estoy cansado.]] [[La sopa está fría.]] [[¿Cómo estás? — Estoy bien.]]
- **ongoing actions**: *estar + -ando/-iendo* (you'll learn it later): [[Estoy comiendo.]]

## Ser or estar?

A simple rule of thumb: **ser = what it is**, **estar = how / where it is**.

| ser (identity, characteristics) | estar (location, state, result) |
|---|---|
| [[Ana es alta.]] — Ana is tall. | [[Ana está cansada.]] — Ana is tired. |
| [[Es médico.]] — He's a doctor. | [[Está en el hospital.]] — He's at the hospital. |
| [[La fiesta es en mi casa.]] — The party is (takes place) at my house. | [[Mi casa está en el centro.]] — My house is in the centre. |
| [[Es aburrido.]] — He's boring. | [[Está aburrido.]] — He's bored. |
| [[Es listo.]] — He's clever. | [[Está listo.]] — He's ready. |

> Location of **things and people** → *estar*. Location of **events** (a party, a concert, a meeting) → *ser*.

Some adjectives change meaning — you'll study more of these at B2. For now remember *aburrido, listo, malo* (ser malo = to be bad; estar malo = to be ill).

**Feelings and moods** almost always take *estar*: [[contento]], [[triste]], [[enfadado]], [[nervioso]], [[preocupado]], [[ocupado]]. But **feliz** is usually used with *ser*: [[Soy feliz.]]

## Useful phrases

- [[¿Dónde está…?]] — Where is…?
- [[Está aquí / allí / ahí.]] — It's here / over there / there (near you).
- [[Está cerca / lejos.]] — It's near / far.
- [[La tienda está abierta / cerrada.]] — The shop is open / closed.
