## Irregular preterites

The most common verbs are irregular in the preterite. Good news: most of them share **one set of endings** (with no accents!) on an irregular stem.

**Endings:** -e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieron

| infinitive | stem | yo | él/ella | ellos |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tener | **tuv-** | [[tuve]] | [[tuvo]] | tuvieron |
| estar | **estuv-** | [[estuve]] | [[estuvo]] | estuvieron |
| andar | **anduv-** | anduve | anduvo | anduvieron |
| poder | **pud-** | [[pude]] | [[pudo]] | pudieron |
| poner | **pus-** | [[puse]] | [[puso]] | pusieron |
| saber | **sup-** | [[supe]] | [[supo]] | supieron |
| hacer | **hic-** | [[hice]] | [[hizo]] (c→z) | hicieron |
| querer | **quis-** | [[quise]] | [[quiso]] | quisieron |
| venir | **vin-** | [[vine]] | [[vino]] | vinieron |
| decir | **dij-** | [[dije]] | [[dijo]] | **dijeron** |
| traer | **traj-** | [[traje]] | [[trajo]] | **trajeron** |
| conducir | **conduj-** | conduje | condujo | **condujeron** |

Stems ending in **-j** drop the *i*: *dijeron, trajeron* (not *dijieron*).

## Ser and ir — identical!

| | ser / ir |
|---|---|
| yo | [[fui]] |
| tú | [[fuiste]] |
| él/ella/Ud. | [[fue]] |
| nosotros | [[fuimos]] |
| vosotros | [[fuisteis]] |
| ellos/Uds. | [[fueron]] |

Context makes it clear: [[Fui al cine.]] (I went) vs [[Fue un día increíble.]] (It was).

## Dar and ver

[[di]], diste, [[dio]], dimos, disteis, dieron · [[vi]], viste, [[vio]], vimos, visteis, vieron — no accents.

## -ir stem-changers (3rd person only)

-ir verbs with a present stem change also change in **él/ella and ellos**: **e → i**, **o → u**.
pedir → [[pidió]], [[pidieron]]; sentir → [[sintió]]; dormir → [[durmió]], [[durmieron]]; morir → [[murió]].

## Meaning changes

Some verbs shift meaning in the preterite: [[supe]] = I found out; [[conocí]] = I met (for the first time); [[no quise]] = I refused; [[pude]] = I managed to.
