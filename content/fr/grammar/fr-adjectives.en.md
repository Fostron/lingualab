## Agreement

Adjectives agree with the noun in **gender and number**. The basic rule: add **-e** for feminine, **-s** for plural.

| | masc. | fem. | masc. pl. | fem. pl. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| regular | [[petit]] | [[petite]] | [[petits]] | [[petites]] |
| ending in -e | [[rouge]] | rouge | [[rouges]] | rouges |

The feminine **-e** often makes the final consonant audible: *petit* (puh-TEE) → *petite* (puh-TEET), *grand → grande*, *français → française*.

## Other feminine patterns

| masculine → feminine | examples |
|---|---|
| -er → **-ère** | [[cher]] → [[chère]], [[premier]] → [[première]] |
| -eux → **-euse** | [[heureux]] → [[heureuse]] |
| -if → **-ive** | [[sportif]] → [[sportive]] |
| -en, -on, -el → double + e | [[bon]] → [[bonne]], [[italien]] → [[italienne]], [[cruel]] → [[cruelle]] |
| -et → -ette | [[muet]] → [[muette]] |
| irregular | [[blanc]] → [[blanche]], [[long]] → [[longue]], [[doux]] → [[douce]], [[frais]] → [[fraîche]], [[gentil]] → [[gentille]] |

Plural: *-s* (silent); **-eau → -eaux**, **-al → -aux** ([[national]] → [[nationaux]]); adjectives in -s/-x don't change.

## Position: usually after the noun

[[une voiture rouge]], [[un livre intéressant]], [[un homme français]], [[une question difficile]].

**BAGS** adjectives come **before** the noun — **B**eauty, **A**ge, **G**oodness, **S**ize: *beau, joli, jeune, vieux, nouveau, bon, mauvais, grand, petit, gros, long*:
[[une jolie maison]], [[un petit chien]], [[un bon restaurant]], [[une grande ville]].

## Beau, nouveau, vieux

They have a special form before a masculine noun starting with a vowel:
| m. | m. before vowel | f. |
|---|---|---|
| beau | **bel** — [[un bel homme]] | belle |
| nouveau | **nouvel** — [[un nouvel ami]] | nouvelle |
| vieux | **vieil** — [[un vieil arbre]] | vieille |

## Meaning changes with position

[[un grand homme]] (a great man) / [[un homme grand]] (a tall man) · [[mon ancien professeur]] (my former teacher) / [[un livre ancien]] (an old book) · [[ma propre chambre]] (my own room) / [[une chambre propre]] (a clean room)

**très** = very: [[très grand]], [[très belle]].
