## Form

**en + present participle**. The present participle comes from the **nous** form minus *-ons* + **-ant**:
parler → nous parl**ons** → [[en parlant]] · finir → finiss**ons** → [[en finissant]] · faire → fais**ons** → [[en faisant]] · prendre → pren**ons** → [[en prenant]]

Three irregulars: **être → étant**, **avoir → ayant**, **savoir → sachant**.

## Uses

The gérondif always has the **same subject** as the main verb.

1. **Simultaneity** — *while doing*: [[Il chante en prenant sa douche.]] — He sings while taking a shower. [[Ne parle pas en mangeant !]]
2. **Means / manner** — *by doing*: [[On apprend en pratiquant.]] — You learn by practising. [[Il est entré en courant.]] — He ran in.
3. **Condition** — *if you do*: [[En partant maintenant, tu arriveras à l'heure.]]
4. **Cause / time**: [[En arrivant à la gare, j'ai vu Paul.]] — When I arrived at the station…

**tout en** + participle stresses simultaneity or contrast: [[Tout en étant riche, il vit simplement.]] — Although rich, he lives simply.

## Gérondif vs present participle

- **gérondif** (*en* + -ant) refers to the **subject**: [[J'ai vu Marie en sortant du cinéma.]] — **I** was leaving the cinema.
- **participe présent** (no *en*) describes a **noun**, often like a relative clause (B2): [[J'ai vu Marie sortant du cinéma.]] — **Marie** was leaving.

The participle in *-ant* is **invariable** (no agreement), unlike adjectives in -ant: [[des enfants souriants]] (adjective) / [[des enfants souriant à leur mère]] (participle).
