Pronominal verbs (with *se*) have four different meanings. Recognising them helps with both meaning and participle agreement.

## 1. Reflexive — the subject acts on itself

[[Je me lave.]] · [[Elle se regarde dans le miroir.]] · [[Il s'est coupé.]] — He cut himself.

## 2. Reciprocal — each other (plural subjects)

[[Ils s'aiment.]] — They love each other. · [[Nous nous écrivons souvent.]] · [[On se voit demain ?]] — See you tomorrow? · [[Ils se sont disputés.]] — They argued.
To avoid ambiguity add *l'un l'autre / mutuellement*: [[Ils se regardent l'un l'autre.]]

## 3. Passive meaning — general facts, rules, habits

[[Ce plat se mange froid.]] — This dish is eaten cold. · [[Ça se voit.]] — It shows. · [[Le français se parle dans 30 pays.]] · [[Ça ne se fait pas.]] — That's not done.

## 4. Essentially pronominal — only exist with se, or change meaning

| only pronominal | meaning changes |
|---|---|
| [[se souvenir de]] — remember | [[douter]] (doubt) / [[se douter de]] (suspect) |
| [[s'enfuir]] — run away | [[apercevoir]] (glimpse) / [[s'apercevoir de]] (realise) |
| [[se moquer de]] — make fun of | [[attendre]] (wait) / [[s'attendre à]] (expect) |
| [[s'évanouir]] — faint | [[rendre]] (give back) / [[se rendre]] (go; surrender) |
| [[se méfier de]] — distrust | [[passer]] (pass) / [[se passer]] (happen); [[se passer de]] (do without) |
| [[s'en aller]] — leave | [[tromper]] (deceive) / [[se tromper]] (be wrong) |

## Se faire, se laisser, se voir + infinitive

These express something **undergone** by the subject (a passive nuance):
- [[Il s'est fait arrêter.]] — He got arrested.
- [[Elle s'est laissé convaincre.]] — She let herself be convinced.
- [[Il s'est vu refuser l'entrée.]] — He was refused entry.
- [[Je me suis fait avoir.]] (colloq.) — I got conned.
