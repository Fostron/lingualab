At B2, you're expected to structure arguments and conversations with a range of markers — not just *et, mais, donc*.

## Structuring an argument (writing)

[[Tout d'abord]] / [[Premièrement]] · [[D'une part… d'autre part]] (on the one hand… on the other) · [[En outre]] / [[De plus]] (moreover) · [[Par ailleurs]] (furthermore, in addition) · [[Ensuite]] · [[Enfin]] · [[Pour conclure]] / [[En somme]] / [[En définitive]]

## Nuancing and contrasting

[[Néanmoins]] / [[Toutefois]] (however — formal) · [[En revanche]] (on the other hand) · [[Certes…, mais…]] (admittedly…, but…) · [[Or]] (now, yet — introduces a decisive fact) · [[Quoi qu'il en soit]] (be that as it may) · [[Il n'en reste pas moins que]] (the fact remains that)

[[Certes, ce projet coûte cher, mais il créera des emplois.]]
[[Il voulait partir. Or, il n'avait pas d'argent.]] — He wanted to leave. Now, he had no money.

## Explaining and exemplifying

[[C'est-à-dire]] (that is) · [[Autrement dit]] (in other words) · [[En effet]] (indeed — gives the reason) · [[À savoir]] (namely) · [[Notamment]] (in particular) · [[Ainsi]] (thus; for example)

## Conversation

[[Bon]], [[ben]], [[bah]] (well…) · [[Enfin]] (well, I mean) · [[Quoi]] (at the end: *you know*) — [[C'était génial, quoi.]] · [[Du coup]] (so, as a result — very common) · [[En fait]] (actually) · [[Franchement]] (honestly) · [[Bref]] (anyway, in short) · [[Tu vois ?]] / [[Tu sais]] (you see? / you know) · [[Genre]] (like — slang) · [[Carrément]] (totally) · [[À la limite]] (if need be, at a push)

[[Du coup, on a décidé de rester.]] — So we decided to stay.

> Don't overuse *du coup* and *en fait* in formal writing — they're spoken markers.
