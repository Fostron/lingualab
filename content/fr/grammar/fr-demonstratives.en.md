## This / that: ce, cet, cette, ces

| | |
|---|---|
| masculine | **ce** — [[ce livre]] |
| masculine before vowel / mute h | **cet** — [[cet homme]], [[cet arbre]] |
| feminine | **cette** — [[cette maison]] |
| plural | **ces** — [[ces livres]], [[ces maisons]] |

French doesn't distinguish *this* and *that* in the adjective. When needed, add **-ci** (here) or **-là** (there):
[[Je préfère cette robe-ci, pas cette robe-là.]]

Time: [[ce matin]] (this morning), [[cet après-midi]], [[ce soir]] (tonight), [[cette semaine]], [[cette année]].

## Ça, ceci, cela

For an unnamed thing or idea: **ça** (everyday), **cela** (formal), **ceci** (this, formal).
[[Qu'est-ce que c'est, ça ?]] — What's that? · [[Ça va.]] — I'm fine / It's OK. · [[Ça coûte combien ?]] — How much is it? · [[Je n'aime pas ça.]] — I don't like that.

## Prepositions of place

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| [[dans]] — in, inside | [[sur]] — on | [[sous]] — under |
| [[devant]] — in front of | [[derrière]] — behind | [[entre]] — between |
| [[à côté de]] — next to | [[en face de]] — opposite | [[près de]] — near |
| [[loin de]] — far from | [[au-dessus de]] — above | [[au milieu de]] — in the middle of |

With **de**, remember contractions: [[à côté du cinéma]], [[près de la gare]], [[en face de l'hôtel]], [[loin des magasins]].
[[Le chat est sous la table.]] [[La banque est à côté de la poste.]]

**ici** (here), **là** (there, also often *here*), **là-bas** (over there).
